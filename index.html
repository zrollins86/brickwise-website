What We Do

Services built for founders

From your first raise to your Series B, we embed alongside your team as the financial partner that grows with you.

Fractional CFO

C-suite financial leadership on a startup budget. Own your numbers, board reporting, investor relations, and long-range strategy without the full-time overhead.

Fundraising Readiness

Pitch-ready financial models, data room structure, investor narrative, and valuation prep — so when VCs come knocking, you're ready to close.

Financial Modeling

Bottom-up revenue models, scenario analysis, cohort projections, and unit economics dashboards that actually tell a story investors believe.

Cap Table & Equity

Cap table modeling, dilution analysis, option pool planning, and SAFEs/convertible note structuring — keeping founders informed through every round.

Runway & Cash Planning

Real-time burn rate tracking, hiring plan stress-testing, and dynamic runway forecasts so you always know your next 18 months with confidence.

Finance Ops & Infrastructure

Build your finance stack from day one — FP&A tooling, reporting frameworks, vendor selection, and the operational infrastructure that scales with you through due diligence and beyond.
