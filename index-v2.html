What We Do
Services built for founders

From your first raise to your Series B, we embed alongside your team as the financial partner that grows with you.

Fractional CFO

C-suite financial leadership on a startup budget. Own your numbers, board reporting, investor relations, and long-range strategy.

Fundraising Readiness

Pitch-ready financial models, data room structure, investor narrative, and valuation prep — ready to close when VCs come knocking.

Financial Modeling

Bottom-up revenue models, scenario analysis, cohort projections, and unit economics dashboards that tell a story investors believe.

Cap Table & Equity

Cap table modeling, dilution analysis, option pool planning, and SAFEs/convertible note structuring through every round.

Runway & Cash Planning

Real-time burn rate tracking, hiring plan stress-testing, and dynamic runway forecasts for the next 18 months.

Finance Ops & Infrastructure

Build your finance stack from day one — FP&A tooling, reporting frameworks, vendor selection, and ops that scale through due diligence.
